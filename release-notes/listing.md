# نصوص صفحة الإدراج في community.obsidian.md

## Short description (≤ 200)
An Arabic-first theme for long-form writing: violet night ink, warm paper and an amber last light. Embedded Naskh and Cairo fonts, careful RTL and a qasida mode.

## Long description (≤ 1000)
Ghasaq (غسق, the first darkness of night) is designed for writing and reading Arabic from the ground up, not translated after the fact. The dark mode, Ghasaq, and the light mode, Asil, share one family of colours: violet-tinted ink, warm paper and an amber accent used sparingly.

- Embedded Noto Naskh Arabic for body text and Cairo for headings and the interface, with real 400 and 700 weights and no network requests.
- Arabic-aware typography: zero letter-spacing, no synthetic italic, a 1.9 line height and no clipped diacritics.
- Mixed-direction notes just work: code, math and Mermaid stay left-to-right, inline code is isolated, and every element sits on the correct side in both directions.
- Qasida mode (cssclasses: qasida) turns two-column tables into classical Arabic verse.
- Contrast is checked against WCAG on every build, and your own accent colour always wins.
- Optional Style Settings support.
