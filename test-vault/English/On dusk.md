# On Dusk

Dusk is the darkness at the start of night, after the afterglow has faded and only a trace of its light remains on the edge of the sky. Writing changes at this hour: sentences grow longer, pauses grow wider, and words take their time before they settle on the page.[^1]

## Ink and Paper

The ink is never quite black and the paper is never stark white; a warm distance between them rests the eye during *long reading*, keeps **bold** text honest, and lets ==highlights== stay readable. Inline `code` sits in the line, and [[On dusk#Ink and Paper|links]] are underlined with an offset. External links look like [this](https://obsidian.md).
